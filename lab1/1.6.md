# 1.6 Most Correlated Features Analysis

Based on the statistical analysis of the first 10 dimensions of the Breast Cancer dataset:

## Result
The pair of features with the strongest correlation is:

*   **Feature A**: `radius_mean`
*   **Feature B**: `perimeter_mean`
*   **Correlation Coefficient**: **0.9979** (approx 1.0)

## Comment on Results

This extremely high correlation (close to perfect +1.0) is **expected** and mathematically inevitable.

1.  **Direct Geometric Relationship**:
    *   The "radius" ($r$) and "perimeter" ($P$) of a circle are linearly related by the formula:
        $$ P = 2\pi r $$
    *   Since cells (nuclei) are roughly circular, as the radius increases, the perimeter increases in direct proportion. The slight deviation from 1.0 is likely due to the irregular (non-perfect circle) shape of the tumors.

2.  **Implication (Multicollinearity)**:
    *   This indicates **Multicollinearity** (đa cộng tuyến).
    *   Ideally, in Machine Learning, we want independent features. Having two features that convey the same information (Size) is redundant.
    *   **Action for Models**: You could safely drop one of these features (e.g., keep Radius, drop Perimeter) without losing significant information, which would help simplify the model (Dimensionality Reduction).
